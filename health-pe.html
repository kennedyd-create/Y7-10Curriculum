Purpose of Health and Physical Education

He oranga ngākau, he pikinga waiora.
Positive feelings in your heart will raise your sense of self-worth.

Health and Physical Education is the study of well-being, health, relationships, human development, and participation in physical activity, games and sport. It supports students' sense of self, their relationships, and lifelong participation in active and healthy lives.

Students develop technical and tactical skills, health and safety knowledge for the outdoors, and an understanding of development in sport, and use this knowledge to make informed decisions.

The two strands

Health
Elements: Physical and mental health; Relationships and society.
Physical Education
Elements: Movement (the moving body, skill acquisition, psychology of movement); Active participation (participating with others, social and cultural influences, outdoor education).

Consultation and sexuality education

Section 91 of the Education and Training Act 2020 requires boards to inform the school community about the content of the health strand. Section 51 allows parents to ask for their child to be released from tuition in sexuality education, which may come up in puberty, sexual health and sexual relationships.

Knowledge and practices

Knowledge sets out the content to teach and how deep to go at each year level. Practices set out the disciplinary skills and applications to teach. Both are teaching requirements, not achievement objectives.

  • They relate but are not an exact match. A practice can connect to more than one knowledge statement, and some knowledge has no matching practice.
  • The teacher decides the direction: start with the knowledge and use the practices to consolidate it, or start with a practice and make the links to the knowledge explicit.
  • Anything marked "e.g." is an illustration. You can substitute examples that better reflect the local environment, community, hapū and iwi connections, student interests or teacher expertise, as long as the intended knowledge and practices are taught.

On this site, a Same for Years 7 and 8 label means the document gives one statement that covers both years of the phase.

The three Health practices

  • Describe a health and well-being situation.
  • Use evidence to explain influences on health and well-being.
  • Select, evaluate, and justify actions to maintain or enhance health and well-being.

These practices sit across both Health elements. Teaching all three is a requirement. The bullets show the expected level for each year and are not exhaustive; you can use other contexts at a comparable level of challenge.

Years 7–8 and 9–10

Phase 3 · Years 7–8

Students extend their understanding of the interconnected factors that influence individual and societal health, including relationships, mental health, and social and cultural influences. They learn more complex technical and tactical skills, use goal setting and reflection to improve performance, and apply mental skills, leadership strategies and sport knowledge to design active experiences.

Phase 4 · Years 9–10

Students develop deeper understanding of health and well-being, including substance use, relationships, mental health and media influences, and critically evaluate information. They apply movement science, skill acquisition, psychology, leadership and performance analysis to refine movement and improve performance in more complex contexts.
